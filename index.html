Welcome, to Goober Gobber Games

Games

Goober Gobber Games is excited to join the board game production industry and we strive to make games that are a blast to play and bring a fun competitive edge to every gameplay session. We are proud to introduce our very first game Rocco's Robots! A game about gathering resources needed for survival through forging new paths, impeding others, and using gained abilities to put yourself ahead on the path to victory!

After being stranded on the only habitable planet within light-years, Rocco was forced to make a decision: give up or survive. Not only did he choose survival, but he repurposed his research Robots into resource gatherers, ready to trek unforgiving terrain. This year though, just gathering wasn't enough, he wanted to make their annual resource gathering trek interesting and pit them against one another! The Robot who could gather their assigned resources first and return home would be the champion!

About Us

Goober Gobber Games

is a Cincinnati, Ohio based company founded by self-proclaimed Goober; Scott Andrews. By day, Scott is a software developer, and by night, he's an avid board gamer. Goober Gobber Games was created out of Scott’s desire to bring joy to those around him and to have a creative outlet for art and storytelling. The company aims to provide players with competitive and strategic games that are also lighthearted and fun. If you choose to play their games, the only requirement is to have a good time, create new memories, and embrace your silly side; be a Goober!
