Games

Goober Gobber Games is excited to join the board game production industry and we strive to make games that are a blast to play and bring a fun competitive edge to every gameplay session. We are proud to introduce our very first game Rocco's Robots! A game about gathering resources needed for survival through forging new paths, impeding others, and using gained abilities to put yourself ahead on the path to victory!

After being stranded on the only habitable planet within light years, Rocco was forced to make a decision: give up or survive. Not only did he choose survival, but this year, when he sent out his repurposed research robots into the unforgiving terrain, he would make their annual resource gathering trek interesting and pit them against one another! The robot who could gather the needed resources first and return home would be the winner would receive an extra special upgrade!
